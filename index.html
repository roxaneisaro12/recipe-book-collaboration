1. Vegetable Fried Rice

A simple and delicious rice dish with fresh vegetables.

Preparation time: 10 minutes

Cooking time: 20 minutes

Servings: 2 people

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of cooked rice
  • 1 carrot, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 cup of peas
  • 2 tablespoons of cooking oil
  • Salt to taste

Preparation Steps

  1. Wash and chop the vegetables.
  2. Heat the cooking oil in a pan.
  3. Add the onion, carrot, and peas, then cook until tender.
  4. Add the cooked rice and salt.
  5. Stir everything together and cook for a few more minutes.
  6. Serve while warm.

2. Simple Pancakes

Soft, homemade pancakes suitable for breakfast.

Preparation time: 10 minutes

Cooking time: 15 minutes

Servings: 4 pancakes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of flour
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup of milk
  • 1 tablespoon of sugar
  • 1 teaspoon of baking powder
  • A little cooking oil

Preparation Steps

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, and baking powder in a bowl.
  2. Add the egg and milk, then mix until smooth.
  3. Heat a lightly oiled pan over medium heat.
  4. Pour some batter into the pan.
  5. Cook until bubbles appear, then flip and cook the other side.
  6. Repeat with the remaining batter and serve.
